---

1. Executive Summary

NSS Connect is a centralized digital platform designed to simplify the management of National Service Scheme activities across colleges and educational institutions.

The platform connects volunteers, Programme Officers, college administrators, and institutional coordinators through one application.

It replaces fragmented administrative processes with a unified digital workflow for volunteer enrollment, event management, attendance tracking, service-hour verification, activity reporting, announcements, certificates, and institutional analytics.

The application must provide a professional, accessible, mobile-first experience while maintaining accurate records, secure access controls, and reliable data separation between institutions.

NSS Connect will initially launch as a pilot-ready application for one institution, using an architecture designed to support multiple institutions without requiring a fundamental redesign.

1.1 Product vision

Make NSS administration simpler, volunteer participation more accessible, and community-service outcomes easier to document and understand.

1.2 Product mission

Connect volunteers and institutions through a reliable digital platform that supports the planning, execution, documentation, and reporting of community-service activities.

1.3 Product tagline

Connect. Volunteer. Transform.

1.4 Brand identity

- Product: NSS Connect
- Brand attribution: Powered by Devzoft
- Primary logo: National Service Scheme logo supplied by the product owner
- Visual direction: Modern, professional, trustworthy, accessible, and community-oriented

The application must not imply official government ownership or endorsement without appropriate authorization.

2. Problem Statement

NSS activities require coordination between volunteers, Programme Officers, educational institutions, and community organizations.

Manual registers, spreadsheets, messaging applications, and disconnected documents can make these responsibilities difficult to manage.

The main problems are:

1. Volunteer information is maintained in different locations.
2. Event announcements are scattered across communication channels.
3. Registration and participant coordination require manual effort.
4. Attendance records are difficult to maintain consistently.
5. Service-hour calculations can contain errors or duplicate entries.
6. Activity reports and photographs are difficult to organize.
7. Institutional reporting consumes administrative time.
8. Volunteers lack a unified view of their participation history.
9. Records may be difficult to verify when corrections are needed.
10. Institutions lack a consistent view of their documented community-service activities.

Proposed solution

NSS Connect provides one centralized application where institutions manage NSS operations and volunteers access their personal participation records.

All important actions must be recorded in a persistent database and processed through permission-controlled workflows.

4. Target Users and Roles

The application must implement server-side role-based access control.

4.1 Super Administrator

Manages the platform.

Permissions:

- Create and manage institutions.
- Assign institutional administrators.
- Suspend or reactivate institutions.
- View platform-level statistics.
- Manage global configuration.
- Review security events and audit logs.
- Manage platform-wide settings.

4.2 College Administrator

Manages one institution.

Permissions:

- Update institution information.
- Create and manage NSS units.
- Assign Programme Officers.
- Review institutional volunteer records.
- View institution-wide events.
- Generate authorized reports.
- Review administrative activity.

4.3 Programme Officer

Manages assigned NSS units.

Permissions:

- Approve volunteer enrollment.
- Manage assigned volunteers.
- Create, publish, update, and cancel events.
- Manage event registrations.
- Record and finalize attendance.
- Review service-hour submissions.
- Review activity reports.
- Publish announcements.
- Issue authorized certificates.
- Generate unit reports.

4.4 Event Coordinator

Manages assigned activities.

Permissions:

- View assigned events.
- Access permitted participant lists.
- Record attendance when authorized.
- Upload event documentation.
- Submit event completion reports.

4.5 Volunteer

Participates in NSS activities.

Permissions:

- Manage permitted personal information.
- Discover events.
- Register for events.
- View registration status.
- View attendance records.
- Track verified service hours.
- Submit activity reports where permitted.
- View achievements.
- Download authorized certificates.
- Receive notifications.
- Request record corrections.

4.6 Public Visitor

Accesses publicly available information.

Permissions:

- Browse public events.
- View approved impact stories.
- Read public announcements.
- Learn about NSS Connect.
- Register for an account.

Public visitors must not access private profiles, internal reports, or attendance records.

The application consists of three primary interfaces.

5.1 Public website

Provides product information, publicly available events, approved activity stories, and account registration.

5.2 Volunteer application

Provides event discovery, registration, personal records, service-hour tracking, reports, certificates, and notifications.

7. Functional Requirements

FR-001: Authentication and Account Management

Priority: P0

The application must provide:

- Email and password registration.
- Secure login and logout.
- Email verification.
- Password recovery.
- Session management.
- Account activation and suspension.
- Server-side authorization.
- Secure privileged-account management.

Acceptance criteria

- Duplicate email registration is rejected.
- Unverified accounts follow the configured verification policy.
- Users cannot assign themselves privileged roles.
- Expired or invalid reset tokens are rejected.
- Protected pages require authentication.
- Authentication failures do not disclose unnecessary account information.

FR-002: Institution and NSS Unit Management

Priority: P0

Authorized administrators must be able to:

- Create institutions.
- Edit institution information.
- Create NSS units.
- Assign Programme Officers.
- Activate or deactivate units.
- View institution-specific statistics.

Acceptance criteria

- Each NSS unit belongs to an institution.
- Unit codes are unique within their institution.
- Programme Officers can access only assigned units.
- Institutions cannot access another institution's private data.

FR-003: Volunteer Management

Priority: P0

The application must support:

- Volunteer enrollment.
- Volunteer approval.
- Profile editing.
- Academic-year information.
- Unit assignment.
- Active and inactive status.
- Participation history.
- Service-hour summaries.
- Enrollment rejection with a recorded reason.

Acceptance criteria

- Volunteer records are linked to a user and institution.
- Unauthorized users cannot edit another volunteer's private profile.
- Institutional staff can access records only within their permitted scope.
- Historical participation remains available when an account is deactivated.

FR-004: Event Management

Priority: P0

Authorized staff must be able to create, publish, update, and cancel events.

Required fields:

- Title.
- Description.
- Category.
- Cover image.
- Organizing institution and unit.
- Start and end dates.
- Venue.
- Registration deadline.
- Participant capacity.
- Eligibility conditions.
- Event visibility.
- Registration status.
- Event status.

Supported categories should include cleanliness drives, tree planting, blood donation campaigns, health awareness, digital literacy, educational outreach, environmental activities, special camps, and other authorized service activities.

Event statuses

- Draft.
- Published.
- Ongoing.
- Completed.
- Cancelled.

Acceptance criteria

- Draft events are not publicly visible.
- Published events appear in authorized event listings.
- Registration deadlines are enforced.
- Cancelled events cannot accept registrations.
- Event updates are validated and recorded.
- Registered participants receive important event notifications when notifications are configured.

FR-005: Event Registration

Priority: P0

Volunteers must be able to register for available events.

The backend must check:

- Authentication.
- Volunteer enrollment status.
- Event publication status.
- Eligibility.
- Registration deadline.
- Existing registrations.
- Available capacity.

When an event is full, the system must apply its configured waitlist policy.

Acceptance criteria

- Duplicate registrations are prevented.
- Capacity cannot be exceeded through concurrent requests.
- Registration status is saved in the database.
- Volunteers can view their registration status.
- Authorized staff can manage registrations.
- Cancellation and withdrawal rules are consistently applied.

FR-006: Attendance Management

Priority: P0

Programme Officers and authorized coordinators must be able to record attendance.

Supported statuses:

- Present.
- Absent.
- Excused.
- Pending verification.
- Finalized.

The MVP must support manual attendance. QR-based attendance is a subsequent enhancement.

Acceptance criteria

- Attendance is linked to an event and volunteer.
- Duplicate attendance records are prevented.
- Only authorized staff can finalize attendance.
- Volunteers cannot directly modify finalized records.
- Corrections require authorization and are audited.
- Attendance history remains available after event completion.

FR-007: Service-Hour Management

Priority: P0

The application must maintain service-hour records independently from registration and attendance.

Each record must contain:

- Volunteer.
- Event or authorized service activity.
- Submitted hours.
- Approved hours.
- Submission date.
- Approval status.
- Reviewer.
- Review timestamp.
- Rejection reason, when applicable.

Workflow

"Draft → Pending Review → Approved or Rejected"

Acceptance criteria

- Only approved hours contribute to verified totals.
- Volunteers cannot approve their own hours.
- Rejected submissions do not contribute to verified totals.
- Duplicate submissions are prevented.
- Approved hours cannot exceed the permitted participation limit.
- Corrections to approved records require an authorized, audited process.
- Applicable institutional requirements can be configured.

FR-008: Activity Reports

Priority: P0

Authorized users must be able to create and submit reports for completed activities.

Required information:

- Activity title.
- Event reference.
- Date and venue.
- Objectives.
- Description.
- Participant count.
- Outcomes.
- Challenges.
- Photographs and supporting documents.
- Author.
- Review status.
- Reviewer comments.

Workflow

"Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Approved or Rejected"

Acceptance criteria

- Drafts can be saved.
- Submitted reports can be reviewed by authorized staff.
- Rejected reports include a reason.
- Approved reports maintain their revision history.
- Private reports remain restricted.
- Public publication requires explicit authorization.

FR-009: Notifications and Announcements

Priority: P1

The application must support in-app notifications for:

- Enrollment decisions.
- Registration confirmation.
- Waitlist updates.
- Event changes.
- Event cancellation.
- Attendance finalization.
- Service-hour decisions.
- Report decisions.
- Certificate issuance.
- Institutional announcements.

Email delivery may be enabled through a configured provider.

Acceptance criteria

- Notifications are linked to their intended recipients.
- Users can mark notifications as read.
- Users cannot read another user's private notifications.
- Failed email delivery does not silently delete the in-app notification.
- Notification delivery status is recorded where supported.

FR-010: Certificates and Achievements

Priority: P1

Authorized staff must be able to issue institution-approved certificates.

Certificate records should include:

- Recipient.
- Certificate type.
- Issuer.
- Issue date.
- Unique reference.
- Related activity, where applicable.
- Certificate status.
- Stored document reference.

Acceptance criteria

- Unauthorized users cannot issue certificates.
- Duplicate certificate references are prevented.
- Issued certificates can be revoked through an authorized process.
- Certificate downloads are permission-controlled.
- Public verification reveals only the information necessary to verify the certificate.

The application must not represent platform-generated certificates as official NSS certificates without the necessary authorization.

FR-011: Analytics and Reporting

Priority: P1

The application must provide:

- Volunteer participation summaries.
- Event statistics.
- Verified service-hour totals.
- Attendance summaries.
- Activity report counts.
- Unit-wise comparisons.
- Academic-year summaries.
- Date-based filters.
- PDF and CSV exports.

Excel export may be included in the MVP if it does not delay core workflows.

Acceptance criteria

- Analytics use persisted application data.
- Pending and approved records are distinguishable.
- Exports respect access permissions.
- Export failures display a useful error message.
- Large reports are generated without exposing unauthorized data.

FR-012: Public Impact Stories

Priority: P1

Authorized staff can publish selected activity stories.

Published stories may include:

- Activity description.
- Date and venue.
- Approved photographs.
- Verified outcomes.
- Participant counts.
- Organizing institution.
- Publication approval.

Only explicitly approved content may be publicly visible.

FR-013: Audit Logging

Priority: P0

Record important actions, including:

- Role changes.
- Institution changes.
- Volunteer approval decisions.
- Attendance modifications.
- Service-hour approvals.
- Report approval decisions.
- Certificate issuance and revocation.
- Account suspension.
- Sensitive report exports.

Audit logs must identify the actor, action, affected record, timestamp, and relevant change metadata.

Passwords, session tokens, and unnecessary personal information must never be recorded in audit logs.

10. Database Requirements

Implement relational database models for:

1. User.
2. RoleAssignment.
3. Institution.
4. NSSUnit.
5. VolunteerProfile.
6. OfficerAssignment.
7. Event.
8. EventRegistration.
9. Attendance.
10. ServiceHourRecord.
11. ActivityReport.
12. ReportAttachment.
13. Announcement.
14. Notification.
15. Certificate.
16. AuditLog.
17. Feedback.
18. Achievement.

Required integrity constraints

- Unique user email.
- Institution-scoped unit uniqueness.
- Unique volunteer registration per event.
- Unique attendance per volunteer and event.
- Unique certificate reference.
- Valid foreign-key relationships.
- Nonnegative hour values.
- Approved hours within the permitted participation limit.
- Valid reviewer references.
- Recorded approval timestamps.
- Institution and unit ownership checks.

Data integrity rules

Important records must not be silently deleted.

Historical attendance, service-hour approvals, and issued certificates must remain auditable even if a user or event becomes inactive.

Use transactions to prevent inconsistent updates during registration, approval, and finalization workflows.

12. Security and Privacy

12.1 Authentication

- Use secure password hashing.
- Protect authentication endpoints with rate limiting.
- Use secure session handling.
- Validate password-reset tokens.
- Protect privileged accounts with stronger authentication where supported.

12.2 Authorization

- Apply server-side role checks.
- Restrict access to assigned institutions and units.
- Validate permissions for every protected operation.
- Prevent role escalation.
- Avoid relying solely on frontend checks.

12.3 Data protection

- Use HTTPS.
- Protect database credentials.
- Restrict file access.
- Use private object storage.
- Apply data minimization.
- Establish retention and deletion policies.
- Maintain tested backups.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive information.

12.4 Indian compliance

Before launch, review applicable Indian data-protection legislation, institutional requirements, student-data handling practices, consent requirements, and any relevant NSS administrative rules.

Do not collect sensitive student information unless it is necessary and appropriately protected.

18. Acceptance Criteria

The MVP is considered complete when all of the following are true.

Authentication

- Registration, login, logout, and password recovery work.
- Privileged roles cannot be self-assigned.
- Protected routes require authentication.

Institution management

- Administrators can create institutions and NSS units.
- Programme Officers can be assigned to units.
- Institutional data remains isolated.

Events

- Officers can create and publish events.
- Volunteers can register.
- Capacity and deadlines are enforced.
- Cancelled events reject registrations.

Attendance

- Authorized staff can record attendance.
- Duplicate attendance is prevented.
- Finalized records cannot be silently changed.

Service hours

- Pending hours are excluded from verified totals.
- Approved hours update totals correctly.
- Approval decisions are auditable.

Reports

- Reports can be drafted, submitted, reviewed, and approved or rejected.
- Authorized exports work.
- Private documents remain protected.

Production readiness

- Database migrations execute successfully.
- Automated core workflow tests pass.
- Critical authorization tests pass.
- Backup restoration has been tested.
- Mobile and desktop workflows function.
- Deployment documentation is available.

21. Definition of Done

The initial release is complete only when:

1. The application runs from documented setup instructions.
2. The database initializes through migrations.
3. An administrator can create an institution and NSS unit.
4. A Programme Officer can be assigned.
5. A volunteer can register and access the application.
6. A Programme Officer can publish an event.
7. A volunteer can register for the event.
8. Attendance can be recorded and finalized.
9. Service hours can be approved.
10. Activity reports can be submitted and reviewed.
11. Authorized users can export reports.
12. Institutional data isolation is verified.
13. Critical security tests pass.
14. The application works on mobile and desktop.
15. Deployment, backup, and recovery procedures are documented.

A static prototype, hardcoded dashboard, or collection of nonfunctional buttons does not meet this definition.

---

22. Final Product Requirement

Build NSS Connect as a complete, secure, multi-institution web application, initially deployed for a single pilot institution.

Prioritize reliable operational workflows, clear role-based dashboards, accurate participation records, and a professional mobile-first interface.

All essential functionality must connect to persistent storage and be validated on the server.

Do not introduce advanced features at the expense of core reliability.
